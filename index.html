03 / Triage

It asks what a dispatcher asks.

A job cannot be dispatched on “someone called about water.” These four answers are what turn a call into a truck rolling.

01

What is happening

Burst supply line, sewage backup, storm intrusion, water heater. The cause decides the crew and the gear.

02

Is water still moving

Active versus stopped changes everything: extraction now, or mitigation tomorrow morning.

03

Can anyone get hurt

Gas, electrical near water, occupants, mobility. The hazard flags ride at the top of the dispatch, not the bottom.

04

Where exactly

Street address, floor, access, callback number. The dispatcher gets a destination, not a story.

09 / Questions

The ones owners actually ask.

Q1How is this different from my answering service?
An answering service takes a message and emails it to you in the morning. This ends with an address and hazard flags on your on-call tech’s phone, tonight. And answering is only the first install — in Growth+ and Scale, the work then moves to the leads and reviews you already paid for. An answering service stops at the message.
Q2Will callers know it is not a person?
Some will. It is built to sound like a calm person taking details, not a phone tree. We will not claim no caller ever notices, because we have not run it in front of your customers yet — and we would rather tell you that on the first call than after your money is on the line.
Q3What if the caller is panicking or hard to understand?
It holds the caller with short questions instead of a script, and it never guesses on a hazard. If the thread breaks, the call goes to your fallback human immediately. A confused handoff beats a confident wrong dispatch.
Q4What if it cannot handle the call?
Straight to your fallback. No voicemail, no hold music, no dead end. You choose who that fallback is and we configure it with the rest of the line.
Q5Do you record calls?
No recording, and no payment data. You get a timestamped log of what was asked, what was answered, and when the dispatch went out. That log is what your monthly review is built from.
Q6What happens in the first month?
We configure the line to your call flow, run the ten test calls with you, and go live only when you approve it. You get the log from day one and a 30-minute review at the end of month one: rings, caught, missed, one fix. Growth+ adds the speed-to-lead, review and reactivation work from the same install.
Q7Why is this priced like a managed service, not an answering service?
Because it is not the same product, and pretending otherwise would be dishonest. An answering service sells you a message. This is the line, managed: configured to your call flow, tested before it goes live, and reviewed with you every month. If all you want is the phone answered, say that on the call - we will quote that scope instead of selling you work you do not need. The figure is written down before anything starts. It is not printed here because it depends on your call volume and your market, and a number invented for a web page would be worse than no number at all.
Q8Do you cold-call people with AI?
No. Inbound only. The AI answers the calls and messages that come to you. It never dials lists, runs outbound campaigns or cold-calls anyone. Speed-to-lead replies by SMS to people who filled in your form and gave consent; a human takes it from there.
